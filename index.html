    Kerala · South India

    Backwaters & tea country

    From Fort Kochi’s layered history to Munnar’s green hills and an unhurried night on the water.

    6 days · 5 nightsKochi → Munnar → Alleppey
    View 6-day itinerary
    1. Day 1Arrive in Kochi; settle in and explore Fort Kochi’s waterfront and heritage lanes.
    2. Day 2Visit Mattancherry and the spice-market area; evening at leisure in Kochi.
    3. Day 3Drive to Munnar through tea country; stop at viewpoints along the way.
    4. Day 4Explore Munnar’s tea gardens and Eravikulam area, subject to park access.
    5. Day 5Travel to Alleppey and board a backwater houseboat for an overnight cruise.
    6. Day 6Disembark after breakfast and depart from Kochi or extend your stay.

    Suggested route; park access and houseboat availability vary by season. Ask us for dates and a tailored quote.

    Rajasthan · North India

    Fortresses & desert skies

    A colorful circuit through Jaipur, Jodhpur, and Udaipur, with time for markets, architecture, and lakeside evenings.

    7 days · 6 nightsJaipur → Jodhpur → Udaipur
    View 7-day itinerary
    1. Day 1Arrive in Jaipur; enjoy an easy first evening in the Pink City.
    2. Day 2Visit Amber Fort, City Palace, and Hawa Mahal; browse Jaipur’s bazaars.
    3. Day 3Drive to Jodhpur; explore the old blue lanes at your own pace.
    4. Day 4Tour Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada; sunset from a rooftop café.
    5. Day 5Travel to Udaipur with a stop en route; unwind by Lake Pichola.
    6. Day 6See City Palace and the old town; take an optional boat ride on the lake.
    7. Day 7Breakfast in Udaipur, then depart or add an extra night.

    Driving times depend on traffic and stops. Entry tickets and optional boat rides can be added to your quote.

    Himachal Pradesh · North India

    Mountain air & cedar trails

    Ease into the hills in Shimla, then follow the mountain roads to Manali for forest walks and big Himalayan views.

    6 days · 5 nightsShimla → Manali
    View 6-day itinerary
    1. Day 1Arrive in Shimla; stroll the Mall Road and Ridge as time allows.
    2. Day 2Explore Shimla’s heritage quarter and nearby viewpoints.
    3. Day 3Drive to Manali via the Kullu Valley; stop for river and valley views.
    4. Day 4Discover Old Manali and the Hadimba Temple area; enjoy a relaxed afternoon.
    5. Day 5Choose a local nature walk or a day trip to Solang Valley, weather permitting.
    6. Day 6Breakfast in Manali, then depart or continue your Himachal journey.

    Hill roads and high-altitude excursions are weather dependent. We’ll adapt the plan to your travel dates.
